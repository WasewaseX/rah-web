## 8. Claim-precision rules (binding on every doc and UI surface, Task 67)

The 2026-10-23 external assessment set two rules; both are now written here and in ROADMAP-AUDIT.md so no future edit can quietly overwrite them:

1. **Pronunciation.** Rah scores the learner's actual recorded speech at the WORD level (measured ASR + deterministic alignment). Until the Azure phoneme-assessment adapter is configured (AZURE_SPEECH_KEY/REGION) AND validated against real learner audio, no document, chip, tooltip, or reply may present the current score as a complete phonetic or accent assessment. The engine-tier chip exists precisely to keep this honest.
